## NIYYAH's separately authored content

The English and Tamil educational explanations in NIYYAH are separately authored study drafts, **not** Tanzil translations and **not** Qur'anic Arabic.

Arabic text should remain immutable, verse numbering should not be changed, and future updates must be compared against the authoritative source before release. The user’s personal reflections and linguistic notes must always remain visually distinct from scripture, with reviewed provenance.
