Hindu · Traditional Sound Practice

Chakra & Bija Sound

A traditional contemplative map from yogic and tantric traditions. Use it as attention and sound practice, not as anatomy or medical treatment.

1 Muladhara · RootBase of the spine · grounding · earth association LAM
2 Svadhisthana · SacralLower belly · flow · water association VAM
3 Manipura · NavelNavel / solar-plexus region · intention · fire association RAM
4 Anahata · HeartCenter of the chest · connection · air association YAM
5 Vishuddha · ThroatThroat · expression · space association HAM
6 Ajna · BrowBrow / inner-attention center · often paired with OM or AUM OM
7 Sahasrara · CrownCrown · traditionally represented by silence in many systems SILENCE
Gentle practice
Sit comfortably. Let the inhale happen naturally. On an easy exhale, chant one syllable without forcing volume or breath length. Try 3 comfortable repetitions, then pause in silence before moving to the next center. Do not hold the breath. Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.
OM / AUM
If using A-U-M, let the sound move naturally from an open “A” toward “U” and finish with the closed-lip “M” hum. The goal is attention and listening, not producing a special frequency.
HeartClear presents these as traditional contemplative associations. It does not claim that chanting a particular syllable cures disease, “activates” a physical organ, or guarantees a spiritual state.
