# HeartClear — Clean Body · Clear Heart source review

**Reviewed:** 28 October 2026  
**Branch scope:** 16 initial lessons; **not** a complete thousand-lesson collection.

## Scripture shown inside the reader

1. **Qur’an 5:6** (ritual washing, full purification and dry-ablution concession): https://quran.com/5:6 ; linguistic comparison https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=5&verse=6 . The *full verse* is shown in Arabic and the English summary is original editorial prose, not a verbatim published translation. The verse should not be relabelled as a generic shower routine.
2. **Qur’an 74:4** (purifying garments): https://quran.com/74:4 ; compare the historical interpretation notes at https://quran.com/74:4/tafsirs/ar-tafsir-al-baghawi . Readers should not be told that one of several possible interpretations is the sole correct one.
3. **Qur’an 91:9** (purifying the self): https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=91&verse=9 . Distinguish spiritual self-purification from soap, bathing or anatomy.
4. **Bible, James 4:8**: KJV full cited verse from https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+4%3A8&version=KJV . Clean hands and a purified heart occur as a pair within a passage about humility and inner conflict.
5. **Bible, Psalm 51:10**: KJV full cited verse from https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+51%3A10&version=KJV . The clean-heart request is a spiritual prayer, not a physiological claim.
6. **Bhagavad Gita 17.14**: original Sanskrit from https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad?field_chapter_value=17&field_nsutra_value=14 ; the verse names bodily purity among disciplines of the body.
7. **Bhagavad Gita 17.16**: original Sanskrit compared with https://www.shlokam.org/gita/gita-17-16.htm and https://www.heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/17.16 where available; the verse addresses disciplines of mind, including purity of intention.

*Qur’anic text provenance:* Tanzil Quran Text, Copyright (C) 2007–2024 Tanzil Project, CC BY 3.0. Preserve exact Arabic words, editorially separate any translation/meaning, and retain attribution. Quran.com was also consulted for verification of cited verse placement. If differences between recensions are discovered, resolve them before publication.

*Bible text provenance:* King James Version (KJV), marked Public Domain by Bible Gateway; no copyrighted modern Bible translations copied.

*Gita text provenance:* original Sanskrit verses; English summaries were newly written and are **not** republished modern translations or commentary.

## Practical knowledge (no invented scripture)

- CDC, Hand Hygiene Frequently Asked Questions, https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/faq/ . Source for soap/water, friction, 20-second handwashing and attention between fingers and under fingernails.
- American Dental Association, Interdental Cleaners, https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/floss . Source for brushing twice daily and between-teeth cleaning daily.
- NHS, Ingrown Toenail and Nail Problems: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/ingrown-toenail/ and https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/nail-problems/ . Source for straight-across toenail trimming and footwear.
- NHS, Personal Foot Care Guide, https://www.merseycare.nhs.uk/patient-leaflets/personal-foot-care-guide . Source for clean/dry feet, especially between toes.
- HeartClear original everyday skin and household commentary is explicitly marked editorial. No source is represented as prescribing a specific medical treatment.

## Product/release limitations

- Readers remain **inside HeartClear**: source references display as citations only, not off-site navigation links.
- The auto-rotating discovery changes only a preview; the current full article does **not** advance, close or restart.
- 16 reviewed articles is a transparent starting point. A future 1,000-article program requires real sourcing, distinct topics, Tamil copy review and content licensing; do not multiply the same lesson into fake records.
- Translations are not labeled as literal unless verified; no religious tradition is claimed to teach identical rituals to another.
- This PR does **not** change existing Qur’an, Bible or Gita players, nor implement automatic next-chapter playback. Those requests need a separate player-focused PR and actual mobile testing.
- This PR does not add audio, a new external service, personal data collection or any modification of books, covers or downloaded media.
