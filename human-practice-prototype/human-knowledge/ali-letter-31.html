A Letter to a Son

A Letter for Every Human

Nahj al-Balagha · Letter 31

Traditionally attributed to Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib, addressing al-Hasan. The Arabic below is the transmitted letter in a historical printed recension, not a surviving manuscript in Ali's own handwriting. The collection was compiled by al-Sharif al-Radi around 1010 CE.

Before you read

This was addressed to one son. You may find yourself in the place of the son as you read. The letter is preserved apart from any modern reflection, so its words and our understanding are never confused.
