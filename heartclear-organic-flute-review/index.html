Technical and human approval must both pass

Technical: original format and hash; peak and RMS; audible clipping; unexpectedly large DC offset; extended dropouts; distracting background noise. Human: real performance, no synthesized/AI sound, no additional oud or drums, no obvious short loops, suitable low-volume headphone character. Rights: actual copyright owner and correct CC0 or CC BY terms, with attribution where needed. None of these is proved by the format alone.
15-minute feasibility: the conservative timeline needs at least 7 min 15 sec of separate ney material and 6 min 30 sec of bansuri, or equivalent additional nonoverlapping approved takes. Existing well-documented single-performer clips are much shorter. Do not manufacture length by slowing them unnaturally or looping a phrase.

Release gate

The stages are: (1) verified licence and provenance, (2) actual original-file headphone audition, (3) waveform review and naturalness check, (4) private 7/10/15-minute WAV mixes, (5) your approval, (6) separate live HeartClear integration. This workspace stops before stage 4 until usable real source files are available.
